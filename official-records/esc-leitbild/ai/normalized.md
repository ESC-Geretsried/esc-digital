## Normative themes

The Leitbild defines the ESC's approved organizational orientation around:

- promotion of ice sports as part of the common good;
- broad participation across recreational, child, youth and competitive sport;
- the importance of adequate sports facilities and sustainable cooperation with stadium/municipal stakeholders;
- playful development of children without inappropriate performance pressure;
- sporting, character and social development of young people;
- making sport compatible with education and working life for adults;
- belonging, fairness, mutual support and social responsibility across age and social groups.
